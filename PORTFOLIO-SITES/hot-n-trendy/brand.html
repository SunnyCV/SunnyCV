Rules

  • Neon is an accent, never a background. Pages stay silver and white; colour lands on badges, meters, prices and glows.
  • Hot is warm, Trendy is cool. Flame colours only for heat and urgency; ice colours only for staying power and dupes.
  • Magazine for features, clean cards for shopping. Cover layouts and collages set the mood; product grids stay tidy and easy to compare.
  • Motion stays smooth. Shimmer, sparkle and shine on hover; nothing that slows a phone down.
  • Dupes, never fakes. Only products sold under their own brand, labelled "similar to".
