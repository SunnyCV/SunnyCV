      Good to Know

      Questions

      How long should I plan to spend?

      Most visitors spend two to three hours. If it’s your first visit, start in the Great Hall and choose two or three galleries. The museum is far too large to see in one day.

      Can I take photographs?

      Yes. Photography for personal use is welcome in the permanent collection, without flash or tripods. Some exhibitions don’t allow photography; look for signs at the entrance.

      Are bags allowed in the galleries?

      Small bags are welcome. Backpacks and bags larger than 11 × 15 inches must be checked at a coat check, free of charge.

      Do my tickets include exhibitions?

      Yes. General admission includes every exhibition on view, plus same-day re-entry.

      Can I walk to the museum through the forest?

      Yes. Ashwood Forest is open from dawn until dusk, and the forest paths lead directly to the museum’s north entrance.
