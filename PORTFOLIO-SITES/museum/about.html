About Ashwood

A Sanctuary of Culture

Founded beside the forest in 1874, Ashwood has spent a century and a half gathering the world’s art and history under one roof.

Our Mission

A museum is more than a place that houses art. It is a tour through time that lasts as long as you want it to.

Ashwood exists so that anyone can stand in front of something made thousands of years ago, by someone they will never meet, and feel understood. We collect, care for and share the art of every culture and every era, from Egyptian gold and Greek marble to Gothic glass and modern canvas.

People arrive with their own eyes and their own ideas: scholars and students, skeptics and dreamers, the professional and the anti-establishment. Here, they observe, think and feel, one exhibit at a time. That is what a museum is for.

The Building & the Forest

A Palace Beside the Woods

Ashwood’s grand Beaux-Arts halls rise at the edge of a forest more than eight hundred acres wide. The architects planned it that way: tall north windows open onto the trees, the Sculpture Court sits beneath a glass roof crossed by branches, and forest paths lead straight to the museum’s doors.

Inside, the bold scale of a nineteenth-century mansion meets the calm of the woods: soaring arches, quiet green galleries and light that shifts with the leaves. It is a place built for wandering, with a clear head and no hurry at all.

Our History

150 Years of Ashwood

1874

A museum for everyone

A group of citizens, artists and collectors charters the Ashwood Museum with a promise to bring art to all people.

1880

Into the forest

The first building opens on the east edge of Ashwood Forest, with three galleries and a single marble statue.

1902

The Great Hall

The grand Ashwood Avenue facade and Great Hall are completed, giving the museum its famous columns and steps.

1926

Treasures of the ancient world

Ashwood’s excavations bring Egyptian and Greek collections home, and the Temple of Artemis facade is raised inside.

1987

The Sculpture Court

A glass-roofed court opens on the forest side, letting trees and sky into the galleries.

2024

The next chapter

A 150th-anniversary renewal reopens the Medieval and Arms & Armor wings and makes every gallery step-free.
