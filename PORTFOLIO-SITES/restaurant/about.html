About Us

A dining room built on two culinary traditions, a lot of patience, and a genuine dislike of rushing a meal.

Where Italian warmth
meets French discipline.

Serenade opened with a simple idea: take the generosity of an Italian kitchen and the precision of a French one, and let them share a single menu. No fusion gimmicks — just two traditions that, it turns out, were never that far apart.

The dining room was designed the same way the menu was written — slowly, and with restraint. Dark beige walls, wine-red details, fine wood floors, and just enough amber light to make you forget what time it is.

We've kept the room small on purpose. Every table gets the same attention a chef gives a sauce that's almost ready — patient, and a little obsessive.

— The Kitchen
